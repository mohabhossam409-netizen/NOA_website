About NOA

NOA is a company for integrated contracting and general supplies. Its landscape work covers building and maintaining green areas, including for resorts and tourist villages.

For villas and private gardens, we design and create outdoor spaces, install irrigation, supply and plant trees, plants and palms, and look after gardens with regular maintenance.
